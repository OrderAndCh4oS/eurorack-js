## Privacy Policy — public page copy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Eurorack JS is a browser-based modular synthesizer operated by OrderandChaos at **eurorackjs.orderandchaos.xyz**. For privacy questions or requests, use **[support@orderandchaos.xyz](mailto:support@orderandchaos.xyz)**.

### Using the instrument

You can use the instrument without an account. Browser-saved patches, workbench scripts and display preferences are stored on your device. A temporary browser draft can preserve your rack while you sign in. Clearing this site's browser storage removes those local records; it does not delete cloud patches.

Audio synthesis and MIDI interaction run in your browser. Cloud patch saves contain module settings, connections and MIDI mappings, together with a rack image. They do not upload recorded audio or workbench source code.

### Sign-in and cloud data

If you request an email sign-in link, Neon creates a one-time verification token and Resend receives your email address and the link to deliver it. Confirming the link signs you in. Using the same verified email as your Google account opens your existing Eurorack JS account. [Resend privacy policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy)

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides an account identifier, email address, name, profile image and email-verification information. We use these to create and identify your account. Our authentication system also stores login tokens, sessions, timestamps, and available IP-address and browser information to maintain and protect sign-in. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, contacts, Drive or Calendar.

When you save or interact with cloud patches, we store the patch content, title, description, chosen creator name, visibility, rack images, revisions, copy ancestry and thumbs-up records. Account and patch data support the features you request; technical records help operate the service, prevent abuse and resolve problems. We do not sell your personal data or use your Google profile for advertising.

For UK data-protection purposes, we use account and patch information to provide the service you request, and rely on our legitimate interests in operating, securing and understanding use of the service for proportionate technical and usage information.

### Private patches, public patches and links

Private cloud patches are accessible through the app to their signed-in owner. Choosing **Public** makes the saved patch, rack image, chosen creator name and copy attribution available to other people. Your creator name may initially come from your Google profile; you can choose a different public name. We do not automatically publish your account email or login credentials.

Other people can copy public patches. Copies retain original creator credit and start private. Making a patch private or deleting it removes public access through this app, but cannot recall copies already made, downloads, screenshots or previews cached by social platforms. Creator attribution may remain in other people's copies; contact us about correcting or removing personal information in that credit.

An unsaved **snapshot link** is different: it contains patch data in the link itself. Anyone who receives that link can load its contents without an account. Treat it as sharing a copy, even if the patch was previously private in your account.

### Cookies and analytics

Sign-in uses cookies on this site's domain to maintain your session and protect the login process. Browser storage also remembers local patches and preferences. Signing out ends your current account session; local patches remain on your device.

The editor loads **Vercel Web Analytics** before sign-in to help us understand visits. Vercel describes this as aggregated analytics without third-party cookies. Its data can include page URLs, referrers, approximate location, browser, device and visit time. Analytics is separate from your account session. See [Vercel's analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

### Service providers

**Google** handles Google sign-in. **Neon** verifies email sign-ins and provides the database for accounts, sessions and cloud patch records. **Resend** delivers sign-in emails. **Vercel** hosts the site and API, stores rack images in private Blob storage, and provides analytics and operational logging. Hosting and authentication may process ordinary request information, including IP addresses. The optional system-architecture page loads its diagram library from **jsDelivr**, which receives a normal web request when that page is opened.

These providers may process information outside the UK. Their privacy information is available from [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Neon/Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/legal/privacynotice), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice) and [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy). Contact us for questions about the providers used for this service.

### Retention and deletion

Cloud patches remain until you delete them or request their removal. Deletion removes the patch from the app and deletes its thumbs-up records. Rack images that are no longer referenced are removed through maintenance cleanup; images still used by saved copies remain. Cleanup and provider backups are not instantaneous.

We retain minimal identifiers and request fingerprints after patch deletion so a delayed save retry cannot recreate the deleted patch. Other copies can retain ancestry and creator credit. Account records, authentication data and operational records are not automatically deleted when you delete one patch. There is currently no self-service account-deletion button: contact us to request account removal or ask what information is held. We retain information where needed to provide the service, handle requests, protect against abuse or meet legal obligations, rather than promising a fixed period that the service does not enforce.

### Your choices and rights

You can save locally without signing in, choose private or public cloud storage, change a patch's visibility, delete your own patches, sign out, and clear this site's browser storage. You can also remove Eurorack JS's Google connection through your Google Account; this does not by itself delete records already held by Eurorack JS.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of personal information, and object to certain uses. Email **[support@orderandchaos.xyz](mailto:support@orderandchaos.xyz)**; we may need to verify your identity. You can also [raise a concern with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

We will update this page when these practices change and show the updated date here.

## Maintainer audit and publication notes — do not publish as page copy

- **Google requirements:** production OAuth needs a public home page describing the app and linking privacy/terms. Privacy must be accessible without login, on the same domain, and describe access/use/storage/sharing of Google data. Set Google branding links to `https://eurorackjs.orderandchaos.xyz/privacy-policy` and `/terms-of-service`. Do not put them behind Vercel deployment protection. [Google OAuth policies](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies), [Google brand verification](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/brand-verification).
- **Transparency review:** the ICO lists controller identity/contact, purposes, lawful bases, recipients, transfers, retention periods or criteria, rights and complaints among the information to provide. The lawful-basis wording above is a proposed operational assessment; the operator must confirm it and actual international-transfer arrangements. No registered company name, postal address, DPO, precise deletion SLA, backup duration or transfer mechanism was supplied, so none was invented. [ICO right to be informed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/).
- **Authentication:** `server/auth.js` requests only Google's default `openid email profile` scopes. Installed Better Auth stores account identifiers/profile, OAuth token fields and session IP/user-agent fields. Session defaults are seven-day expiry with refresh, but database-record deletion is not the same as cookie expiry. `user.deleteUser.enabled` is not configured; no account-deletion UI or comprehensive administrative deletion command exists.
- **Token handling:** current Better Auth configuration does not enable `account.encryptOAuthTokens`; installed SDK default stores OAuth tokens as database values without application-level token encryption. Do not claim end-to-end encryption, that Google tokens are never stored, or that data cannot be read by the operator/providers.
- **Retention gaps:** `db/cleanup.mjs` must actually be scheduled/run. It makes unreferenced preview associations eligible after 24 hours and clears application rate buckets older than one day; those are eligibility rules, not guaranteed deletion times. Request tombstones have no TTL. There is no full account/like/auth/tombstone erasure sweep or documented provider-backup expiry. Manual account requests need an operational procedure covering all of these, plus attribution in other users' copies.
- **Analytics correction:** `src/index.html` unconditionally loads `/_vercel/insights/script.js`; there is no consent/opt-out control. A `beforeSend` hook now strips query strings and fragments from analytics event URLs and normalizes saved-patch IDs. Do not say the site has no analytics. No custom analytics events were found. Vercel describes aggregate data and no third-party cookies, but that does not by itself establish the operator's legal basis or settle all cookie/privacy obligations. The sanitization covers documented event URLs, not HTTP referrers or arbitrary future custom-event fields. Do not claim that no private information can ever reach the hosting provider.
- **Ancillary CDN:** `src/system-architecture.html` imports Mermaid from jsDelivr. It is not part of synthesizer/cloud saving but is within the deployed site.
- **Deletion/sharing:** application patch deletion is owner-only and removes likes; copied content and root/parent credit deliberately survive. Public name fields can be edited on one's own record, but provenance snapshots cannot be edited through the public API. The terms' public-copy grant is proposed new product policy, narrowly scoped to operating and sharing through this service; it is not a claim that all patches are available under an existing Creative Commons or software licence.
- **Implementation sources inspected:** `server/auth.js`, `server/handler.js`, `server/patch-service.js`, `db/001-patches.sql`, `db/cleanup.mjs`, `src/index.html`, `src/js/ui/patch-library.js`, `src/js/patches/cloud-client.js`, `src/js/patches/local-patch-store.js`, `src/js/patch-script/storage.js`, and installed Better Auth provider/schema/options source.
