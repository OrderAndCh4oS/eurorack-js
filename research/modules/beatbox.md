# Beatbox — triggered mouth percussion

> Parked by the user after audition, 17 November 2026. See the [archive decision](../topics/vocal-beatbox-parked.md). Historical implementation notes follow.

Status: implemented and locally validated, 2026-11-17. User requested separate vocal and beatbox modules.

## Current contract

BEATBOX is the compact 16 HP percussion module. Its eight knobs are Sound,
Tune, Shape, Decay, Snap, Pressure, Breath and Level. Hit auditions the selected
sound; Wrap selects cyclic or clamped sound addressing. It has eight CV/trigger
inputs and one audio output. The full parameter and voltage contract, shared
DSP decisions and implementation plan are in the two-tier upgrade below.
BEATBOX PRO is a separate 28 HP module; neither replaces the other.

Ten sounds: B kick, t hat, tss open hat, Pf snare, K rim, inward K snare,
tongue click, lip pop, kch crash and M hum. Tune now transposes every sound;
its range is +/-12 semitones including CV. The earlier Hum-only pitch control
is superseded. Existing patches keep the `pitch` parameter and `vOct` port
names; defaults retain zero transposition.

Each instance owns its physical state. Hits choke the preceding hit with a
1 ms bridge. Use separate instances and an external mixer for overlapping hits.
There is no sustaining Gate, voice mode, internal sequencer or hidden second
voice. The kernel gate stays false and the module is silent before its first
hit. All sounds are generated by the pinned physical model; no training or
sample library is involved. Prepared pressure impulses may still sound when
live Pressure is zero.

### Compact panel and voltages

BEATBOX (`beatbox`), 16 HP, voice, module-color-eight. Eight knobs:
Sound 0..9 integer (0), Tune -12..12 semitones (0, existing `pitch` key),
Decay .25..4 times (1), Snap 0..1 (.5), Shape 0..4 (0), Pressure 0..22 (8),
Breath 0..1 (.15), Level 0..1 (.8). Hit button and Wrap switch (0=Clamp,
1=Wrap; default Clamp retains existing sound addressing).
Inputs: Trig 0..10 V >=1 rising; Sound CV +/-5 V, 2 sounds/V; Accent 0..5 V,
1..1.5 gain captured per hit; Tune V/Oct +/-5 V, knob/12+CV clamped +/-1 oct;
Shape CV +/-5 V adds .8/V; Pressure CV +/-5 V adds 4 cmH2O/V;
Decay CV +/-5 V scales knob by 2^(CV/2.5), clamps .25..4;
Snap CV +/-5 V adds .2/V, clamps 0..1. All normals zero. Sound, Decay, Snap
and Accent capture at Trig; Tune, Shape, Pressure, Breath, Level remain live.
Output +/-5 V mono. Trigger and output LEDs. Display names sound and shows
Tune, Decay, Snap and Clamp/Wrap. Sound address is floor(knob + 2*CV), then
clamp 0..9 or Euclidean modulo 10. -1 wraps to 9, 10 to 0. Changing Wrap or
Sound CV while a hit is playing selects the next hit; it never retriggers.

### DSP decisions and limitations

Tune is bandlimited varispeed of freshly generated physical-model audio,
not an added oscillator, stored PCM library or a claim that noise has a
fundamental. It moves every sound's spectrum, including Hum. Ratio .5..2
bounds CPU cost; source is generated on demand in fixed small chunks with
preallocated circular history. Use 64-tap Blackman-windowed sinc kernels,
precomputed phase/cutoff tables and interpolation, with cutoff reducing as
read speed rises. A fixed source-sample delay protects causality; target
added latency <2 ms. Audio-rate parameter changes are slew-limited over
2 ms. No FIR/table generation, WASM compilation, heap growth or temporary
array views in process. Unlike a granular delay shifter, this avoids repeated
transients and long windows. Unlike tract scaling, it tunes clicks and noise
without violating the physical model's numerical stability floor.

Varispeed also changes time. Decay retimes the native post-onset gesture
ends/releases, with the initial Tune ratio compensated at trigger time where
possible. Later pitch envelopes naturally alter remaining time. Decay is not
a promise to lengthen the acoustic ring of an already finished click.
Snap changes native articulator release speed and release ramps; initial
prepared pressure remains intact. Preserve the 1 ms transition on retrigger.
Decay/Snap affect the current native score in a reserved vector and update
its expiry; they do not rerun setup in the audio callback. Canonical retimed
score is retained for non-accumulating PRO articulation changes.

### Implementation Plan

- Existing working checkout; no parallel workers. Keep unrelated compositions.
- Shared scope: CHIMERA kernel ABI for score timing/physical modifiers and a
  reusable streaming bandlimited reader; common percussion DSP used by both
  module definitions. VOCAL retains its existing ABI and behavior.
- Tests before DSP: Wrap boundaries/negative CV/exact trigger capture, all-sound
  tuning vs spectrum, Hum octave accuracy, Decay tail duration, Snap response,
  reset/independence, trigger/Choke priority, all knobs/CV, no NaN, +/-5 V,

- Update retained patch JSON/defaults and strict UI contracts; no migrations.
- Validation: focused module/kernel/reader/contracts/queue/patch tests; native
  tests; strict voltage matrix for both; build; real browser/audio/capture and
  CPU check; full npm test. Rebuild WASM provenance and previews.

### Sources and evidence for the extension

Existing linked CHIMERA primary sources above remain pinned. Additional local
source review 2026-11-17: `control/score.rs` exposes gesture intervals, release
ramps and speed; `phonetics/beatbox.rs` has fixed bursts and variable duration
hats/crash/Hum; `voice/human_airway.rs` has independent glottal/constriction/teeth
noise gains and stability floors; `anatomy/human.rs` has lips, tongue, nasal and
throat geometry. Calibrated body changes reconstruct the physical network,
so continuous Body/Size is deliberately outside this shipping scope.

and [sinc implementation](https://github.com/libsndfile/libsamplerate/blob/master/src/src_sinc.c),
accessed 2026-11-17: support bandlimited interpolation and variable-rate stream
consumption. Our bounded table/reader is an app adaptation, not a port or a
