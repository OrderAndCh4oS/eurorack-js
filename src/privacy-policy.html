Privacy policy

How we handle information when you play, save and share with Eurorack JS.

Last updated · 13 November 2026

You can use the instrument without an account. Cloud patches can be private or public. Public patches can be copied by other users, with original creator credit.

Who runs Eurorack JS

Eurorack JS is a browser-based modular synthesizer operated by OrderandChaos at eurorackjs.orderandchaos.xyz. OrderandChaos is responsible for the personal information described here. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@orderandchaos.xyz.

Playing without an account

Audio synthesis and MIDI interaction run in your browser. Browser-saved patches, workbench scripts and display preferences stay in this site's storage on your device. A temporary browser draft can preserve your rack while you sign in.

Cloud saves contain module settings, connections and MIDI mappings, together with a rack image. They do not upload recorded audio or workbench source code. Clearing this site's browser storage removes local records; it does not delete your cloud patches.

Google sign-in and saved data

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides an account identifier, email address, name, profile image and email-verification information. We use this information to create and identify your account. Our authentication system stores login tokens, sessions, timestamps and available IP-address and browser information to maintain and protect sign-in.

We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, contacts, Drive or Calendar. We do not sell your personal information or use your Google profile for advertising.

Cloud features store your patches, titles, descriptions, chosen creator name, visibility, rack images, saved revisions, copy ancestry and thumbs-up records. We use account and patch information to provide the service you request. For UK data-protection purposes, we rely on providing that service for this processing, and our legitimate interests in operating, securing and understanding use of the service for proportionate technical and usage information.

Private patches, public patches and links

Private cloud patches are accessible through the app to their signed-in owner. Choosing Public makes the saved patch, rack image, chosen creator name and copy attribution available to other people. Your creator name may initially come from your Google profile. You can change it in My profile to update your name across existing account patches and cloud copy credits. We do not automatically publish your account email or login credentials.

Other people can copy public patches. Copies start private and retain original creator credit. Making a patch private or deleting it removes public access through this app, but cannot recall copies already made, downloads, screenshots or previews cached by social platforms. Creator credit may remain in copies; contact us about correcting or removing personal information in that credit.

An unsaved snapshot link contains patch data in the link itself. Anyone who receives it can load the contents without an account. Treat it as sharing a copy, even if that patch was previously private in your account.

Cookies and analytics

Sign-in uses cookies on this site's domain to maintain your session and protect the login process. Browser storage remembers local patches and preferences. Signing out ends your current account session; local patches remain on your device.

The editor loads Vercel Web Analytics before sign-in to help us understand visits. Vercel describes this as aggregated analytics without third-party cookies. Its data can include page paths, referrers, approximate location, browser, device and visit time. Analytics is separate from your account session. Read Vercel's analytics privacy information.

Service providers

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Neon provides the database for accounts, sessions and cloud patches.
  • Vercel hosts the site and API, stores rack images in private Blob storage, and provides analytics and operational logs.
  • The optional system-architecture guide loads its diagram library from jsDelivr, which receives a normal web request when you open that guide.

Hosting and authentication providers may process request information such as IP addresses, including outside the UK. Their privacy information is available from Google, Neon/Databricks, Vercel and jsDelivr. Contact us for information about the providers used for this service.

Retention and deletion

Cloud patches remain until you delete them or request their removal. Deletion removes a patch from the app and deletes its thumbs-up records. Unreferenced rack images are removed through maintenance cleanup; images still used by saved copies remain. Cleanup and provider backups are not instantaneous.

We retain minimal identifiers and request fingerprints after deletion so a delayed save retry cannot recreate a deleted patch. Other copies can retain ancestry and creator credit. Deleting one patch does not delete your account or authentication records.

There is currently no account-deletion button. Email support@orderandchaos.xyz to request account removal or ask what information we hold. We retain information while needed to provide the service, handle requests, protect against abuse or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can save locally without signing in, choose private or public cloud storage, change visibility, delete your patches, sign out and clear browser storage. You can remove Eurorack JS's Google connection through your Google Account; this does not itself delete records already held by Eurorack JS.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of personal information, and object to certain uses. Contact support@orderandchaos.xyz; we may need to verify your identity. You can also raise a concern with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

We will update this page when these practices change and show the updated date here.
